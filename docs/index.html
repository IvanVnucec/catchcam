Catchcam

About

Catchcam is a plug and play speed camera detector that works completely offline. It's similar to popular Waze or Radarbot apps, but with total privacy in mind - nobody tracks where you are, and no internet connection is needed. Just plug it into a car's USB socket and drive.

It features:

  • Built-in speaker and LED for alerts,
  • Already packed with 66,000+ known speed camera world-wide, with more coming soon each day,
  • Easy updates - just plug into your PC and drag-and-drop the latest software.

How it Works

Catchcam uses a GPS module to determine your speed and position. It then compares your position to a database of known speed camera locations. If you're approaching a speed camera, Catchcam will alert you with a voice message spefiying the camera speed limit and a visual LED alert. Speed camera database is updated regularly from the OpenStreetMap.

Each day there is about 50 new speed camera added to the database. You can check a map below to see speed camera coverage in your area.

Installation

  1. Grab a USB-C cable and plug connect a car USB socket with Catchcam
  2. Put the Catchcam on the windscreen dash panel with the top side facing up. You can also place it on a seat or elswhere where you find it convinient.

Device Update

Device update with the newsest speed camera positions are as straightforward as using an ordinary USB thumb drive. The steps are as follows:

  1. Download latest catchcam.uf2 file.
  2. Grab a USB-C connector and connect one end to your PC.
  3. Press and hold Update button located on the right side of device with a paperclip or toothpick and connect the other end of the USB-C cable.
  4. The device should appear in your file explorer as a USB thumb drive.
  5. Drag and drop a newly downloaded catchcam.uf2 file to the device and wait for device to restart itself. It should take about 10-30 seconds.
