Catchcam

About

Catchcam is a plug-and-play speed camera detector that operates entirely offline, ensuring complete privacy. Unlike apps like Waze or Radarbot, Catchcam requires no internet connection and doesn’t track your location. Simply plug it into your car’s USB port and drive.

Key features include:

  • Built-in speaker and LED for real-time alerts
  • Preloaded with 66,000+ global speed camera locations, with new ones added daily
  • Easy updates - just plug into your PC and drag-and-drop the latest software.

How it Works

Catchcam uses a built-in GPS module to track your speed and location. It cross-references your position with a database of over 66,000 speed camera locations worldwide. When approaching a camera, Catchcam alerts you with a voice message specifying the speed limit and a flashing LED.

The speed camera database is updated daily from OpenStreetMap, with approximately 50 new cameras added each day. Explore the map below to view coverage in your area.

Installation

  1. Connect Catchcam to your car’s USB socket using a USB-C cable.
  2. Place Catchcam on the windshield dashboard with the top side facing up, or position it conveniently on a seat or elsewhere.

Device Update

Updating Catchcam with the latest speed camera database is as easy as using a USB thumb drive:

  1. Download the latest catchcam.uf2 file.
  2. Connect one end of a USB-C cable to your PC.
  3. Press and hold the Update button on the right side of the device (use a paperclip or toothpick) while connecting the other end of the USB-C cable.
  4. The device will appear as a USB thumb drive in your file explorer.
  5. Drag and drop the downloaded catchcam.uf2 file to the device and wait for it to restart (10-30 seconds).
